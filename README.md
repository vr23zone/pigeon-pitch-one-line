Entry for the make.fun competition [Pigeon Pitch, One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf).

A city pigeon pitches its startup to investors in a single line of at most
200 characters. The program in `main.ts` writes that one line to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> Investors, I don't need your seed money — I eat that. I need scale:
> 40,000 couriers who navigate by instinct, never strike, and redecorate
> your rivals' statues for free.

The joke: "seed money" is literal food to a pigeon, so the pitch flips the
usual ask — not capital, but distribution — and sells the flock's real
assets: homing instinct, zero labour disputes, and free statue
"redecoration" aimed at the investors' rivals.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`.
